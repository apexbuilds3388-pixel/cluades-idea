Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Avyo builds websites for local businesses. This page explains what information avyo.net collects, why, and what we do with it. In short: we only use your information to work with you, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • Form details you send us. When you ask for a free preview, we get what you type in: your name, business name, phone, email, type of business, current website, any notes, the photos you upload and the name of whoever referred you (if you add one). Your photos are only used to build your site.
  • Basic visit statistics. We use Google Analytics to see how many people visit, which pages they view and which buttons they tap (like call or get a preview). This uses cookies and does not tell us who you are.
  • Payment details. Card payments go through Stripe, and Venmo and Apple Pay payments go through those apps. We never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your website preview and answer your questions.
  • To build, host and update your website if you become a client.
  • To send receipts and handle payments.
  • To understand which parts of this site help people, so we can improve it.

What we don't do

We don't sell, rent or trade your information. We don't add you to marketing lists without asking.

Who helps us run the site

A few trusted services handle parts of the work: Netlify (hosting and form submissions), Google Analytics (visit statistics), Stripe (card payments) and our email forwarding provider. They only get what they need to do their job.

Your choices

  • Want us to delete your information or stop contacting you? Email ryley@avyo.net or text 209-801-4891 and we'll take care of it.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach Ryley Reed at ryley@avyo.net or 209-801-4891.
